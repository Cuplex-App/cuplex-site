This policy explains what data the Cuplex app for iPhone (“Cuplex” or “the app”) processes, where it is stored, who can see it and how you can exercise your rights under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”). It also covers this website.

In short

  • Cuplex has no servers of its own: your data lives on your iPhone and in your personal iCloud account. We don't receive it and can't read it.
  • With your partner, data travels only through iCloud sharing, and only the data you choose to share.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics tools or third-party SDKs, no sale of data.
  • You can export or delete everything from the app at any time.

3. Data about health and sex life

Diary and cycle data are special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR). Cuplex processes them only with your explicit consent, which you give at first sign-in with a switch separate from the 18+ confirmation. You can withdraw it at any time from Settings → Privacy → Withdraw consent: withdrawing leads to deleting your data, because the app cannot work without consent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

4. Apple Health (HealthKit)

Only if you allow it on the iOS permission screen, Cuplex:

  • writes moments to Health (as sexual activity, with a yes/no for protection) and cycles (menstrual flow);
  • reads your sexual activity and menstrual flow history from Health when you choose “Import from Health”.

Data read from Health becomes moments and cycles in your diary and follows the same rules. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose and is never sold or disclosed to third parties. You can change permissions in Health → profile → Apps → Cuplex.

Data imported from Health is saved in the diary like everything else: on your iPhone and in your iCloud, with the encryption described in section 5. We do not receive it and do not use it for any other purpose.

5. Where data is stored and who can see it

  • On your iPhone the store uses the highest iOS file protection: it can't be read while the phone is locked. The app only opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
  • In your iCloud (CloudKit, private database): the diary syncs across your devices. All diary fields, except the technical record identifiers, are stored with CloudKit encryption: the keys are kept in iCloud Keychain and, according to Apple, neither Apple nor Cuplex can read the content. iCloud is provided by Apple under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • With your partner: once you link up, shared moments move into an iCloud zone shared between the two of you, encrypted the same way. Rating, mood and private notes never enter the shared zone: your partner only receives the “I've rated” signal and sees the ratings only once you've both rated. Your cycle reaches your partner only as you choose (everything, predictions only, or nothing).
  • Cuplex (us) has no access to any of this data. There is no server or database of ours that receives it.

Cuplex does not transfer data outside the European Union, because it does not receive any. iCloud and other Apple services may run on servers outside the EU: those transfers are made by Apple, under its terms with you and the GDPR safeguards described in its privacy policy.

6. Notifications

  • Alerts from your partner: when your partner updates the couple's diary, iCloud sends you a notification with fixed, generic text (for example “There's something new in your diary.”), with no diary content. It goes through Apple's notification service.
  • Fertile window and reminders: these are local notifications, scheduled and shown by your iPhone; by default their text is generic.

You can turn them off in the app's Settings → Notifications or in iOS.

7. What Cuplex does not do

  • No advertising, cookies, advertising identifiers or tracking across apps and websites.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. (The invitation QR code is scanned with the other iPhone's Camera app.)
  • No automated decision-making or profiling.

If you chose in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated statistics and crash reports for the app, which contain no diary content. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

If you take part in the TestFlight beta, Apple shows us in App Store Connect the feedback, screenshots and reports you choose to send and, if you were invited by email, your name and address. Please do not send screenshots with real data; we delete them once read.

8. Legal bases

  • Data about health and sex life: explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Running the app and the partner sharing you request: performance of the service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Crash reports, Apple statistics, the TestFlight beta and replies to emails: legitimate interest in fixing the app and answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Cuplex Plus purchases: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); payment is processed by Apple.

9. How long

Data stays until you delete it. Settings → Delete all data removes your entries, ratings and cycles from the phone and from iCloud. Entries your partner wrote stay with them; if you unlink, your partner keeps their copy of the shared moments. Deleting the app does not delete data from iCloud: use “Delete all data” first, or manage iCloud storage from iOS settings. Reminders and local preferences disappear with the app.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time. You exercise most of these directly in the app: edit or delete any entry, export everything with Settings → Export everything (JSON), delete everything with Delete all data. Since we don't hold your data we can't look it up or delete it for you, but we can help you do it: write to {{mailto:privacy_email}}.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it), or the authority in the country where you live.
