Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use Cuplex?

An iPhone with iOS 18 or later, an Apple ID and iCloud turned on. For couple features you need two iPhones, one each, with two different Apple IDs. You must be an adult.

How do I link my partner?

From Couple → Invite your partner, choose which past moments to share and Cuplex shows a QR code. Your partner scans it with their iPhone's Camera (or you send them the link), chooses what to share in turn and accepts. The first person to accept becomes your partner.

My partner can't see my moments or ratings

Sync goes through iCloud and usually takes a few seconds, but it can't be forced. On both phones check that iCloud is on, there's a connection and Low Power Mode is off, then open Cuplex. Remember that ratings only show once you've both rated.

If the problem persists, open Settings → Sync status on both phones and use “Copy the report”: it contains only states and numbers, no diary content. Paste it into an email to support.

How do I unlink my partner?

From Couple → Unlink partner. Choose which moments to keep in your diary. Ratings, mood and private notes always stay with whoever wrote them.

What does my partner see of my cycle?

You decide in Cycle → What your partner sees: everything, predictions only, or nothing. If you don't track a cycle, turn off I track my cycle.

How do cycle alerts work?

You'll find them in Settings → Notifications → Cycle alerts: period coming, period late and expected ovulation, with the notice and the time of day you prefer. They are local notifications computed from the predictions, with no server, and they cover your own cycle only if I track my cycle is on. As a couple you can also get an alert for your partner's cycle if they share predictions. Some alerts are off by default.

Notifications say too much (or too little)

By default notifications use generic text that gives nothing away. You can change this in Settings → Notifications with “Notifications with generic text”, and turn off partner alerts, the fertile window alert and reminders there.

Importing from Apple Health finds nothing

iOS doesn't tell apps whether read access was denied. Check in Health → profile → Apps → Cuplex that reading sexual activity and menstrual flow is on, then try again from Diary → ⋯ → Import from Health or from Cycle.

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings → Export everything (JSON) creates a file with all your data. Settings → Delete all data removes it from the phone and from iCloud. Deleting just the app does not remove data from iCloud.

Can I make Cuplex more discreet?

Yes: in Settings → Discretion you can pick a neutral icon. The app always asks for Face ID or your passcode and hides itself in the app switcher; widgets show only numbers and can be turned off.
