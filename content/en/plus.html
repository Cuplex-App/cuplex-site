The three plans

Three ways to get Plus, all bought through the App Store. Whoever pays unlocks Plus for the linked partner too.

Lifetime

€49.99 one time only

A single purchase, no renewals.

Good to know

  • Prices are those planned for Italy: in the app you always see the ones for your country, and they may change before release.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period, from iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Apple handles payments, refunds and invoices.
  • One pays, both benefit: if the partner who paid leaves the couple or the subscription ends, the other goes back to the free version without losing any data.
  • If you change iPhone, Restore Purchases on the Plus screen brings your plan back.
  • Without Plus you never lose your data: a weight or a test already recorded stays, and a cycle pause in progress can always be ended.
